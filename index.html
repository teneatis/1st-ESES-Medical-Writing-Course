1st ESES Medical Writing Course

1 - 10 June 2026
12 hours of lessons spread over 4 days (3 hours each)
Welcome to the ESES Medical Writing Course, organized by the Education Committee of the European Society of Endocrine Surgeons (ESES). This course has been designed to provide ESES members with essential knowledge and practical skills in study design, medical writing, scientific publication, and research dissemination, supporting the development of high-quality academic and clinical research. The course is offered free of charge and will take place on Monday 1st June, Wednesday 3rd June, Monday 8th June, and Wednesday 10th June, from 18:00–21:00 CET. Upon successful participation, attendees will receive a certificate awarded by the ESES Education Committee.
